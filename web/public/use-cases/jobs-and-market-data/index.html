Job search and market data APIs for AI agents

Search public job boards, labor-market listings, company data and economic indicators as structured JSON.

These read-only commands help an agent find job listings and public market information, with source-specific filters and pagination documented on each site page.

Verified sources

arbeitnow

arbeitnow.com

Arbeitnow: a job board for jobs in Germany (remote, visa sponsorship, English speaking). This CLI searches and filters jobs, lists a company's jobs, reads the free public job-feed API, opens job details and lists hiring companies.

arbeidsplassen-nav

arbeidsplassen.nav.no

arbeidsplassen.nav.no is NAV's Norwegian public job board. This CLI searches job ads with filters, sorting and paging, opens individual ads, and reads a few informational pages. The site is server-rendered, so most commands parse HTML.

Machine-readable commands · 5 verified commands

arbetsformedlingen

arbetsformedlingen.se

Platsbanken (Arbetsförmedlingen, the Swedish Public Employment Service job board): search job ads by keyword and municipality, read full ad details, list popular occupations, look up location and occupation taxonomy IDs, and get search suggestions.

remoteok

remoteok.com

RemoteOK (remoteok.com) is a remote job board. This CLI searches and filters remote job listings, reads the public JSON jobs API, opens job details, lists related jobs, company jobs and country jobs, and lists the job tag catalog. No login is needed.

Machine-readable commands · 5 verified commands

usajobs

usajobs.gov

USAJOBS (usajobs.gov), the U.S. federal government's job board. The CLI searches federal job announcements with filters, sorting and pagination, fetches job details, suggests keywords and locations, lists recruiting events, and saves jobs for signed-in users.

Machine-readable commands · 6 verified commands

tyomarkkinatori

tyomarkkinatori.fi

Työmarkkinatori (tyomarkkinatori.fi), the Finnish public employment services portal. This CLI searches open job postings and reads their details, searches public trainings and services and reads course and service details, lists occupation info pages, and reads the code lists the site's filters use (municipalities, regions, working time, languages and so on).

fred-stlouisfed

fred.stlouisfed.org

FRED (Federal Reserve Economic Data, St. Louis Fed): search economic time series and read their metadata and latest observations, and browse categories, tags, releases, release tables, the release calendar and data sources.

eia

eia.gov

U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA): energy statistics through the public Open Data API v2 (routes, data queries, facets, legacy series IDs), dnav data tables, Today in Energy articles, daily and weekly fuel prices, glossary and FAQs.
